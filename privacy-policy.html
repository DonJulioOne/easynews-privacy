Privacy Policy App: Easy Newsreader Friend Developer: Julian Kroll Last updated: March 8, 2026 1. Introduction Easy News Reader Friend is a free RSS news reader app. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy. 2. Data Collection Easy News Reader Friend does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app does not require registration or login. 3. RSS Feeds The app loads publicly available RSS feeds from third-party news sources. These requests are made directly from your device to the respective news providers. We have no control over the data practices of these third-party providers. 4. Internet Access The app requires internet access solely to load RSS news feeds. No user data is transmitted to our servers, as we do not operate any servers. 5. Third-Party Services To load RSS feeds, the app may use CORS proxy services. These proxies only forward the requested RSS feed content and do not store personal data. 6. Cookies and Tracking The app does not use cookies, tracking technologies, or analytics tools of any kind. 7. Advertising The app contains no advertisements and does not use any advertising networks. 8. Children's Privacy The app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. 9. Changes to This Policy We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. 10.
